Our Journey

✿

2012 — Founded in Mylapore

BNR Event Planners began as a two-person wedding styling studio serving Chennai families.

2016 — First 100 Weddings

Crossed our 100th wedding milestone and expanded into corporate event production.

2019 — Expanded to Hyderabad

Opened a second studio to serve Telangana families and temple trusts across the region.

2024 — 500+ Events & Counting

Today, a team of 20+ specialists and 50+ trusted vendors bring every celebration to life.
