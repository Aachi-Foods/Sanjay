From a Family Atelier

Eleven Years of Silk, Gold & Jasmine

BNR Event Planners began in 2015 in a small Mylapore courtyard, styling temple weddings for family and neighbours. What started as a labour of love — hand-tying jasmine garlands, sketching kolam patterns for mandapams — has grown into Chennai's most trusted name in South Indian luxury event design.

We still believe the old ways hold the most meaning: a muhurtham timed to the minute, a nadaswaram note that stirs the whole hall, a banana-leaf feast served with warmth. Our craft is simply making these traditions feel considered, editorial, and entirely your own.
