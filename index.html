Who We Are

A Full-Service Luxury
Event Planning House

BnR Event Planners is a full-service luxury event planning company dedicated to transforming ambitious visions into refined, unforgettable experiences. From intimate private celebrations to grand destination weddings and large-scale corporate galas, our team brings together creative direction, meticulous logistics, and an obsessive attention to detail.

We believe true luxury lives in the details others overlook — the weight of a menu card, the pacing of an evening, the way light falls across a reception at golden hour. As your dedicated luxury event planner, we curate every element of your celebration, coordinate a hand-selected network of premier vendors, and oversee execution so that you experience your own event as a guest, not a manager.
